“A Bold Step of Faith”

When we started Forgiven Church Unusual, we had nothing but faith, vision, and a handful of dedicated hearts.

Miraculously, doors swung open, hearts gathered, and provision appeared from unexpected sources.

Just because we don’t have a way and the path isn’t clear doesn’t mean God doesn’t have a way.

Every seed we plant through our giving helps build God’s house and transform lives within our communities. We are believing God for your house.
